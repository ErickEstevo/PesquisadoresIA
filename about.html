What does an Artificial Intelligence Researcher do?

An Artificial Intelligence Researcher is a professional dedicated to the investigation and development of new methods, models, algorithms, and applications capable of enabling computers to perform tasks that normally require human capabilities.

Unlike professionals who only use existing artificial intelligence tools, the researcher seeks to understand complex problems and develop new solutions. Their work may involve mathematics, statistics, programming, data science, machine learning, and experimentation.

Research can take place in universities, research centers,technology companies, specialized laboratories, and organizations that use artificial intelligence to solve scientific or industrial problems.

Research and innovation

The researcher seeks to transform complex problems into questions that can be investigated, tested, and solved through computational methods.

What is part of research work?

AI research combines theoretical study, programming, experimentation, and analysis of results.

Problem definition

Identifying a relevant issue that can be investigated using computational methods.

Bibliographic research

Study of scientific articles, existing methods, previous results and new approaches.

Development

Implementation of algorithms, models, or computational experiments to investigate the proposed hypothesis.

Testing and analysis

Evaluation of the results obtained, comparison between methods, and identification of limitations.

Scientific communication

Presentation of results through reports, scientific articles, conferences, and other academic means.

Required skills

Essential knowledge for the profession

Research in artificial intelligence requires a combination of technical knowledge and investigative skills.

Mathematics

Algebra, calculus, probability, and statistics are important for understanding various AI models.

Programming

Programming skills allow you to create experiments, implement algorithms, and work with data.

Critical thinking

It is necessary to analyze hypotheses, interpret results, and identify limitations in the methods used.

Collaboration

Research projects often involve professionals from different fields and areas of expertise.

Why is this profession important?

Research today to develop the technologies of tomorrow.

This research contributes to expanding knowledge about artificial intelligence and developing new ways to use computer systems to solve problems.

In addition to technological development, research also allows for the investigation of limitations, risks, and more responsible ways of using artificial intelligence systems. Therefore, the researcher participates both in the creation of new technologies and in understanding their potential impacts.
